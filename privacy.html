Wedding Memories 2026 Privacy Policy

Jaztine and Mary Ven use Wedding Memories 2026 to collect photos and videos from their wedding guests.

Guest uploads

When you submit an upload, the service receives the photos and videos you choose, their filenames, and any name or message you enter. Original files may contain information embedded by your device, such as the capture time or location. The upload service uses a protected identifier derived from the IP address to limit abuse. Guests can upload without signing in to Google.

How the app uses Google Drive

The folder owner authorizes the app using Google Drive's per-file permission. The app uses that access to check the selected folder, save guest uploads, and confirm that uploads completed. It stores the files in the owner's private Wedding Memories folder. The guest page does not display uploaded files publicly. The owner can revoke access from their Google Account.

GitHub Pages hosts the wedding site, Cloudflare processes the uploads, and Google Drive stores the files.

Questions and deletion requests

To ask about an upload or request deletion, contact the couple at the same contact you use for the wedding invitation. Include enough detail to identify the upload.
